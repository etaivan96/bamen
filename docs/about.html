About Bamen

Built for background artists.

We’re building Bamen around the work of turning a storyboard into backgrounds for a finished episode.

Why Bamen exists

A storyboard tells you where a shot belongs. A SketchUp scene tells you what can be seen from that angle. Bamen brings those decisions together in the panel, where you can save the camera and return to it when the episode changes.

How we develop it

Our focus is the everyday route from page import to panel registration, camera capture, and render. We use feedback from artists to make those steps easier to discover and repeat.
