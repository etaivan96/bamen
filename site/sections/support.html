Common questions

Can I use SketchUp 2020?

No. The current Bamen extension requires SketchUp 2025 Desktop on Windows. Other versions need separate compatibility testing.

Where do I find the HUD?

Sign in to Bamen and open SketchUp 2025. Look under Extensions → BG Authoring for the HUD controls. If SketchUp was open during installation, restart it.

What should I do if my save is unconfirmed?

Open the history icon next to the folder in Bamen and choose Inspect Original Save. Copy the exact status text if you need to report the problem.
